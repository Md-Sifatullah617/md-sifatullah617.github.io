# sifatullah.me

The personal-brand hub for Md Sifatullah. Its job is to position him as a technical
leader and founder, with Manobsheba as the flagship proof of that. Audience, in order:
professional network and peers (reputation), then partners/investors/NGOs evaluating
Manobsheba, then inbound clients and opportunities.

## Language

**Brand hub**:
This site. Not a "portfolio" in the junior-developer sense (a wall of apps to prove
employability) — a hub that establishes reputation and points to everything else.
_Avoid_: portfolio, personal website

**Manobsheba**:
A healthtech platform bringing basic healthcare to rural citizens of Bangladesh,
co-founded by Md Sifatullah as CTO. In private beta as of 2026. Public site:
manobshebabd.com.
_Avoid_: Manobsheba App, the healthtech project

**Health Care Agent Point (HCAP)**:
The local, in-person delivery point for Manobsheba. A trained agent at an HCAP operates
the Manobsheba platform on behalf of a rural citizen who would not otherwise have
digital access — so the citizen gets the service without needing their own device or
connectivity.
_Avoid_: agent, kiosk, branch

**Venture**:
A company or product Md Sifatullah co-founds or leads, as distinct from employment (a
job) or client work. Manobsheba is a venture; the unnamed software company will be one.
_Avoid_: side project, startup

**Case study**:
A dedicated page on one piece of work — problem, his role, architecture, outcome —
written to be sent as a standalone link. Distinct from a project card, which is a
one-paragraph teaser on the landing page.
_Avoid_: project page, portfolio entry
