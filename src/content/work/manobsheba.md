## The Health Care Agent Point model

Manobsheba reaches people through a **Health Care Agent Point (HCAP)** — a local,
in-person delivery point. A trained agent at an HCAP operates the Manobsheba
platform on behalf of a rural citizen who would not otherwise have digital
access. The citizen gets the service without needing their own device or
connectivity; the agent is the interface.

This case study is held at a deliberately restrained depth while Manobsheba is in
private beta. It will be expanded when the venture goes public.
